Hacks

  • Eight hours at Hamad is nothing to fear. Look for the indoor garden with the trees. →
  • If you're four or more adults, compare MoMA's annual membership with ticket prices. →
  • Without a Canadian visa you can still see all the main sights from the US side. The best head-on view of the Horseshoe is from Canada, though. →
  • Restaurants add sales tax (~9%) and a tip (18–22%) on top of menu prices. Budget about a third extra.
  • The subway is far cheaper than taxis: $3 a ride vs ~$60 for a cab from Downtown to Midtown in the evening. Pay by tapping a bank card or phone (OMNY).
