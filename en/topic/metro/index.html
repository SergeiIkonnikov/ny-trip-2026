Day 1 · 4 Nov

A $150 dinner and our first subway ride

◍ FoodⓂ Subway☺ People

First surprise: everything is very expensive, and tax and tip go on top of the menu price. Dinner for three came to $150.

Makar fell asleep right in the café, so soundly that Natasha had to carry him for a bit. A taxi to the hotel would have been about $60, so we took the subway, sleeping child and all. It took us a while to work out which train we needed, but we got there.
