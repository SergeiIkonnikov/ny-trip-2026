How do you get to Carnegie Hall

Carnegie Hall opened in 1891. Tchaikovsky conducted at the opening: he crossed the Atlantic for it.

There's an old American joke. A man stops a musician on the street: "How do you get to Carnegie Hall?" The musician says: "Practice, practice, practice." The hour with a piano two days earlier was exactly about that.

The theatre Disney saved

The New Amsterdam Theatre, home of Aladdin, opened in 1903. In the 1910s and 20s it hosted the famous Ziegfeld Follies. Later it became a cinema, fell into decay and stood closed for years. In the 1990s Disney restored it, and it's been a theatre again ever since.
