3–11 November 2026 · Natasha, Makar, Sergey

New York, November 2026

Eight days, the three of us: Natasha, Makar and me. Written for family and friends.

In early November the three of us flew to New York. The occasion was a serious one: Makar was playing at a competition in Carnegie Hall. We assumed the whole trip would revolve around one concert.

It turned out differently. The concert happened, and Makar took first place, but in our memory it sits alongside other things. The bus to Washington and our guide David. A waterfall we spent twelve hours on the road for. A subway we took a long time to understand. A dinner where the waiters sing between the tables. The city turned out bigger than the reason we came.

We wrote all this down mostly for ourselves, so that in a few years we can open it and remember. That's why the prices here are real, November 2026, and why it keeps the small things that are usually the first to go. You can read it day by day, as a story. Or by topic: that's where we put what didn't fit into the story.
