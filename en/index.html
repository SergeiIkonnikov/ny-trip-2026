New York, November 2026

Eight days, the three of us: Natasha, Makar and me. Written for family, but everyone is welcome.

We flew to New York because Makar was playing at a competition in Carnegie Hall. That was the reason. The trip turned out bigger than the reason: Washington, Niagara, a subway we took a long time to understand, MoMA, an ice rink, a musical, and a dinner where the waiters sing.

You can read it day by day, as a story. Or by topic: that's where the things that didn't fit into the story live. The prices are real, November 2026. In a couple of years they'll be interesting to look at.
