New York, November 2026

Eight days, the three of us: Natasha, Makar and me. Written for family, but everyone is welcome.

We flew to New York because Makar was playing at a competition in Carnegie Hall. The trip quickly became much more than that: Washington, Niagara Falls, a subway we took a while to figure out, MoMA, an ice rink, a musical and a dinner where the waiters sing.

Everything is here by day, by topic and on a map. Prices are real, as of November 2026: it will be fun to look at them again in a few years.
