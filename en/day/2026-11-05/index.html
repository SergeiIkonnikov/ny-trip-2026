Arlington National Cemetery

☺ People

America's main military cemetery, where more than 400,000 people are buried. It holds John F. Kennedy's grave with its eternal flame and the Tomb of the Unknown Soldier, guarded around the clock.

⌖
Arlington National Cemetery
12:30

Soldiers in ponchos, and Lincoln

◐ Art

The Korean War Veterans Memorial: 19 steel soldiers in ponchos, as if on patrol through the bushes.

Next to it, the Lincoln Memorial, built like a Greek temple: 36 columns, one for each state at the time of Lincoln's death, and inside a seated Lincoln nearly 6 metres tall. Martin Luther King gave his "I Have a Dream" speech on these steps.
