Washington in a day

One day in the capital, one good guide, and one woman who was in all of our photos.

1.

The bus to Washington and our guide David

On day two we left New York. A bus tour to Washington, booked through GetYourGuide. Our guide was David. His English wasn't the best, and the tour was excellent. It happens: a man knows what he's talking about, and that's enough.

3.

Soldiers in ponchos, and Lincoln

Then the Korean War Veterans Memorial. Steel soldiers in ponchos walk through the bushes, and it feels like one of them is about to turn around.

Next door is Lincoln. He is sitting down. Unlike us, he's in no hurry.

The woman who was everywhere

All day the same woman kept walking into our shots. At the cemetery, in a red top. At the White House. On the pavement.

We don't know her. By the evening she was almost part of the group. If you recognise yourself, thank you: the day would have been duller without you.
