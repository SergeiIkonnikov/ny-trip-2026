3.

Cave of the Winds

Then Cave of the Winds: a lift down, then wooden boardwalks right under the falls. Here the ponchos are yellow.

Nothing much happened that day. But the falls are impressive. Twelve hours on a bus for them — no regrets.

Tip. Without a Canadian visa you can still see all the main sights from the US side. The best head-on view of the Horseshoe is from Canada, though.
