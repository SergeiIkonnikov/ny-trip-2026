The subway broke, run for the bus

We left early in the morning. That morning the subway broke down. We started the trip unable to figure it out, and ended it with the subway unable to figure us out.

We ran for a bus. On the plus side, from its window we filmed the outskirts, not just the postcard centre. That's New York too; people just rarely look at it.
