2.

The Doha layover and the forgotten bag

We had eight hours in Doha. First we sat down in one of the countless waiting areas and went to freshen up. Then we got up and moved on. The red Comme des Garçons bag with all our documents stayed behind on the seats.

We noticed it was gone about half an hour later. We spent the next half hour running around the airport, wide-eyed.

The bag had waited for us. The heart attack, thank God, also agreed to wait a while longer, and we went back to wandering the airport. It's much more pleasant with your documents.

Sleeping on the grass

The airport has proper places to sleep. We chose the grass.

The grass is artificial, the trees are real. You lie under a tree, inside an airport, in the middle of a desert, somewhere between Cyprus and America. Very cool.

Tip. Eight hours at Hamad is nothing to fear. Look for the indoor garden with the trees.
