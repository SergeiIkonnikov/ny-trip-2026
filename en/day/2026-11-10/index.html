Last day: the bridge, Katz's and Aladdin

The last day. We tried to fit in everything we hadn't got to yet.

1.

The 9/11 Memorial

We started the last day at the 9/11 Memorial. Two square pools where the towers stood. The water falls into a smaller square, and you can't see the bottom. Names are cut along the edges. Everyone here speaks quietly.

The last slice

After the musical, at half past nine, we dropped into Joe's on Broadway. A pizzaiolo at the oven, celebrity photos on the wall, Spider-Man on the sign. We took the pizza back to the room and finished it there. Our last dinner in New York came out of a cardboard box, and I think that's exactly right.
