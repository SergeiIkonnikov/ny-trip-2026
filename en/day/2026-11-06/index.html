Natasha's hack

Regular tickets for six would have been $120. Natasha found out that an annual membership costs $90 and lets you bring five guests. We saved $30 and got a card for a year. We have nowhere to use it, but it's nice.

Tip. If you're four or more adults, compare MoMA's annual membership with ticket prices.
